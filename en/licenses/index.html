01How we credit

Pixel Detective is built with open-source software and with content the operator created or is properly licensed to use. Where a license requires attribution, we list the author and license on this page.

The fonts, music, sound effects, images, and icons in the app are tracked file by file in an internal asset register that records each source and the permitted use, and we update it with each app update.

05Images and case content

The graphics, including case, scene, suspect, clue, and case-truth images, the app icon, and the splash screen, were created or edited by the operator. The cases’ stories, characters, dialogue, and text were also written for this project and have no connection to real people, organizations, or events.

The Google and Apple logos on the sign-in screen are official brand assets provided by each company. We use them only on sign-in buttons, without changing their shape or color, following each company’s sign-in button brand guidelines. The rights to these logos belong to Google LLC and Apple Inc.

06Rights inquiries

If you believe content in the app infringes your rights, or you find something missing from these license notices, send us the file name or where it appears, the basis of your rights, and an email address for our reply.
