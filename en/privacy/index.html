03How we collect information and our legal basis

We collect information when you:

  • Create a guest account or sign in with Google or Apple
  • Set a nickname, investigate and solve cases, or use AI interrogation
  • Agree in the consent window before sending your first AI question
  • Open the app in a place where the law requires an age check and the app store provides your age range
  • Buy or restore a paid product, or when a refund is applied
  • Report an AI answer or email us for support or deletion
  • Use the service and our servers log requests for security, cost control, and error handling

We process information to provide the service you signed up for, based on consent you give (such as AI interrogation consent), to meet our legal obligations, and to keep the service safe. Statistics for balancing and improving features are produced only in aggregate form that doesn’t identify individuals.

04Cookies, advertising IDs, and tracking

The app doesn’t use cookies and doesn’t collect or use advertising identifiers (Android Advertising ID or Apple’s IDFA). We haven’t added ad networks, standalone analytics tools, or third-party trackers to the app, and we don’t track your activity in other apps or on other websites.

The Google sign-in process and the Google Sign-In SDK included in the app may, according to Google’s published privacy manifest, process your name, email address, phone number, other data types, approximate location, user and device identifiers, and other usage data for sign-in and service analytics. This isn’t used for ad tracking, and the app doesn’t ask for location permission or advertising identifiers.

We don’t process personal information for targeted advertising. If we ever add advertising or analytics tools, we’ll update this policy and let you know in the app or on this site before we do, and we’ll ask for consent where the law requires it.

05How AI interrogation works

When you ask a suspect a question, your question and the conversation context needed for the answer go through our servers to the Anthropic API, and the answer comes back the same way. Before sending, our servers check your access to the case and how many questions you have left.

Before you send your first question, the app explains this and asks for your consent. Your question is sent only after you tap [Agree and Send Question]. If you don’t agree, you can’t use AI interrogation, but everything else works as usual. We keep a record of the version of the notice you agreed to and when, and we’ll ask again if the notice changes significantly.

We send the Anthropic API only your question, the case setup, and the conversation context it needs. We don’t send your email or nickname because they aren’t needed to create answers, but anything you type into a question is sent along with it.

We save successful questions and answers separately from usage records so you can pick up the conversation on the same account, and so we can check for spoiler leaks or inappropriate answers. We don’t store the text of failed requests. Only your account and the minimum number of people needed to run the service can access saved conversations.

AI answers are generated automatically and may be inaccurate or out of context. Please report problematic answers with the in-app report button, and don’t include information that could identify you in your questions.

06How long we keep information

We keep your account, profile, game progress, XP, operations records, and report data while your account exists, and delete them when your account is deleted, except where the law requires us to keep something or we need it to handle a dispute. Guest accounts follow the same rules.

  • Account and game data, saved AI questions and answers, and report records: until your account is deleted
  • Encrypted Apple refresh token: until your account is deleted. It’s used to revoke Apple authorization when you delete your account and is then deleted with your account
  • AI usage records: until your account is deleted. They don’t include the text of questions or answers
  • AI interrogation consent records: until your account is deleted
  • Age check information: not stored. It’s discarded right after the check
  • Server logs: the platform’s default retention period. They don’t contain question or answer text or case prompts
  • Purchase and refund records: even after account deletion, we keep records of payments and delivery of goods, and of contracts and order cancellations, for 5 years, and records of consumer complaints and disputes for 3 years, separately from other data, as required by Korea’s Act on the Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce, and then delete them
  • Support emails: until your request has been handled or you ask us to delete them. Records of purchase-related complaints and disputes are kept separately for 3 years as described above
  • Device settings: until you delete the app or clear its storage

Under Anthropic’s standard API policy, API inputs and outputs are deleted from Anthropic’s systems within 30 days of being received or generated. If they’re flagged for a usage policy violation, inputs and outputs may be kept for up to 2 years, and the period may also differ where the law requires. Anthropic doesn’t use customer content received through its commercial API to train its models. For the latest details, see Anthropic’s API retention article.

We delete electronic files so they’re hard to recover, along with the records linked to your account ID. Any copies left in backups are removed on the backup rotation schedule while access to them is restricted.

How to refuse optional transfers, and what happens if you do

If you sign in as a guest, no sign-in information goes to Google or Apple. If you don’t use AI interrogation or submit AI answer reports, no question or alert information goes to Anthropic or Resend. If you don’t buy paid products, no transaction information goes to RevenueCat. So you can refuse these optional transfers by not using social sign-in, AI interrogation, AI reports, or paid purchases. You just won’t be able to use those features; everything else works as usual.

Storing your account and game records is necessary to provide the service, so it can’t be refused while you use the service. If you don’t want this processing, you can stop using the service and delete your account as described in Delete Your Account and Data.

Our database is in the Seoul region of the Republic of Korea (ap-northeast-2), and subprocessors published by Supabase may take part in security, support, and network services. See the latest list in Supabase’s subprocessor list.

09How we protect information

We apply the following measures according to the risks of the information we handle.

  • Encryption in transit and session-token-based authentication
  • Database access control (Row Level Security) and the principle of least privilege
  • XP, case solving, and access to the next case decided on our servers
  • Keeping admin secret keys and AI API keys out of the app
  • Question limits per account, case, and suspect, hourly limits, and cost limits
  • Admin-only access to report records and operations data
  • Identity checks for deletion requests, deletion of accounts and linked data, and clearing the app’s sign-in state

10Your rights and how to use them

You can ask to access, correct, or delete your personal information, ask us to stop processing it, and withdraw consent. You can change your nickname or delete your account in the app’s Profile tab, or email us if you can’t sign in.

  1. Email your request to pixeldetective.app@gmail.com.
  2. We’ll confirm your identity as needed so no one else’s account is deleted.
  3. We’ll handle your request within the time required by law and tell you the result or any reason we can’t.

To withdraw your AI interrogation consent, simply stop using AI interrogation. No more questions will be sent after that, and you can have your saved questions, answers, and consent record deleted by deleting your account or emailing us.

You can find the in-app deletion steps and how to request deletion by email in Delete Your Account and Data.

11Users under 14

Korea’s Personal Information Protection Act requires consent from a legal guardian to process the personal information of children under 14. Instead of a guardian consent process, this service is for people aged 14 and older. We don’t offer the service to children under 14 or knowingly collect their information.

We don’t ask your age when you sign up. However, in places where the law requires it (such as Texas in the United States), we check the minimum age using the age range the app store (Apple) provides when you open the app, and we restrict use if you’re under 13. This information is used only for the check and isn’t stored.

If we learn that we’ve processed a child under 14’s information without a legal guardian’s consent, we’ll delete the account and information without delay. A parent or guardian can email us to check or request deletion.
