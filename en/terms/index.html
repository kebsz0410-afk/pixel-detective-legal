03Accounts, sign-in, and linking

You can sign in as a guest or with Google or Apple.

  • A guest account is also created as a unique account on our servers, and your case records and question counts are saved.
  • If you link to a social account that has no Pixel Detective records yet, you keep using your existing guest records.
  • If the social account already has other records, it won’t be linked and you’ll see a notice; the records you’re using stay as they are. We don’t offer a way to automatically merge records from different accounts or move them from one to the other.
  • If you delete the app or reset your device before linking a social account, you may not be able to get back into your guest account.

You’re responsible for keeping your device and sign-in method secure, and you should tell us if you think someone else is using your account.

04The game and access to cases

You investigate scenes, collect clues and belongings, question suspects, and submit your deduction. Whether a case is solved, its grade, XP, and access to the next case are decided based on our server records.

The conditions for unlocking the next case are shown in the app. A free case unlocks right after you solve the previous free case and our servers confirm it. Paid cases can be played once you buy them, whether or not you’ve solved earlier cases. You can revisit completed cases in your case records, and reinvestigation follows “Paid products and payments.”

If we find a content error or safety problem, we may temporarily suspend access to a specific case, and we’ll keep progress you’ve already made as far as possible.

05XP and in-game records

XP and detective ranks are virtual records of your game progress. They have no cash value and can’t be transferred or sold to other users.

XP is awarded in amounts set by our servers when you solve a case, based on your grade, and when you collect all the clues and belongings in a case. XP already awarded for a case isn’t awarded again. If you get a higher grade, you receive only the difference, and if your grade goes down, we don’t take back XP you’ve already received.

We may correct records created by improper requests or system errors after checking them. We don’t accept XP values that the app sends on its own.

06Paid products and payments

The app sells the following paid products. All of them are one-time, non-consumable unlocks with no subscription, auto-renewal, or recurring payment. While your purchase remains valid and the service is running, they have no expiry date (an internet connection is required). However, you can no longer use them if access is revoked because of a refund or payment cancellation, or if your account is deleted.

  • Reinvestigation pass (per free case): Unlocks reinvestigation for that case and raises its AI interrogation limit from 3 to 6 questions per suspect (applied once, at your first purchase). Reinvestigating again after purchase doesn’t add or reset AI questions. You can buy a reinvestigation pass from the case record screen only after completing that case.
  • Paid case access (per case marked as paid): Lets you play that case. It includes 6 AI questions per suspect and unlimited reinvestigation, so you don’t need a separate reinvestigation pass.

The following apply to purchases and use:

  • Only members with a linked Google or Apple account can buy paid products. Guests need to link an account before buying. Purchases belong to your Pixel Detective app account and apply on both iOS and Android when you sign in with the same app account. Apple Family Sharing isn’t supported.

  • Payments are handled by each store’s in-app purchase system, and we don’t receive card numbers or other payment details.

Refunded or cancelled purchases can’t be restored, and game progress from before you deleted your account isn’t restored.

The following apply to cancellations and refunds:

  • Payment cancellations and refunds follow the procedures of the store where you bought (App Store or Google Play) and the applicable laws. For users in Korea, this includes the Act on the Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce.

07AI interrogation

AI interrogation creates answers from fictional suspects based on your questions. The number of questions one account can send to each suspect in a case is shown in the app. By default it’s 3 successful questions per suspect, and 6 for free cases with a reinvestigation pass and for paid cases. We may change these limits and conditions when needed to run the service, and we’ll let you know in the app if we do.

Because your questions are sent to an outside AI (Anthropic) to create answers, we ask for your consent in the app before you send your first question. If you don’t agree, you can still use every feature except AI interrogation.

Answers are generated automatically, so they may be inaccurate, inconsistent with the story, or unpleasant. AI answers aren’t advice you can rely on for real-life decisions, including legal, medical, or financial ones. Please report problematic answers with the report button on the speech bubble. We review reported answers and, if needed, adjust the interrogation settings or restrict the feature.

Successful questions and answers are saved until you delete your account so you can continue the conversation and so we can check quality and safety. Don’t include personal information, passwords, verification codes, or sensitive content in your questions.
